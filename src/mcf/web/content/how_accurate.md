We tested the tool on the history of $projects real open-source projects. A forecast is one prediction of when the next 10, 25 or 50 work items (not bugs or epics) will be done.

1. Pick a past date in a project. Use only the work done up to that date.
2. Make the forecast.
3. Compare it with the date the work was really done.

- **$cases** past forecasts checked (**$no_forecast** more had too little history to forecast)
- **$done_85 of 100** done by the 85% date (target: 75 to 95 of 100)
- **$later_cap** finished later than the 85% date

So the 85% date is often too early. Use the 95% date when a late finish costs a lot. The result is published as it is, although it missed the target.
