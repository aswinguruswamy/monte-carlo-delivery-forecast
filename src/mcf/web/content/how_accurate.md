We tested the tool on the history of real open-source projects. A forecast is one prediction of when the next 10, 25 or 50 work items (not bugs or epics) will be done.

1. Pick a past date in a project. Use only the work done up to that date.
2. Make the forecast.
3. Compare it with the date the work was really done.

The target: 75 to 95 of 100 forecasts are done by the 85% date.

**Latest test – $full_projects projects (full data)**
- **$full_cases** past forecasts checked (**$full_no_forecast** more had too little history to forecast)
- **$full_done_85 of 100** done by the 85% date: $full_verdict

**First test – $projects projects**
- **$cases** past forecasts checked (**$no_forecast** more had too little history to forecast)
- **$done_85 of 100** done by the 85% date: $verdict

So the 85% date is right about 3 times in 4, at the low edge of the target. Use the 95% date when a late finish costs a lot. Both results are published as they are.
