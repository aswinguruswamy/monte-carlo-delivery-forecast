The tool does not use one average pace. It replays your team's real good days and bad days many times.

1. Count how many work items the team finished on each of the last $window_days days.
2. Make one try: for each future day, pick one of those past days at random and use its count.
3. Add the counts until all the work left is done. That day is the finish date of this try.
4. Do $runs tries. Each try gives a different finish date.
5. Sort the finish dates. The 50% date (P50) is the day by which half of the tries finish. The 85% (P85) and 95% (P95) dates work the same way. Give the 85% date (P85) to your sponsor or client.

Story points are not needed for these dates. The velocity date uses story points, for comparison.
